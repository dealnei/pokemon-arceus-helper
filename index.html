How this works: a local, rules-based scoring model compares Pokémon types, shared weaknesses, offensive coverage, physical vs. special roles, base stats, and your chosen strategy. It uses native types as a proxy for offensive STAB coverage and does not verify each possible move or predict exact turn order. Recommendations are opinions, not guaranteed battle outcomes.

Sources & accuracy: Pokémon availability and basic evolution strings are read from the open-source pkDex Hisui dataset; stats from PokémonData. PLA-specific methods and locations cross-referenced with Gamer Guides and Pokémon Database.

The guide retrieves open-source encounter data when first opened and caches it locally for later visits. Caught, favorites and team selections are saved locally in your browser. Mythical encounters and some postgame Pokémon need special conditions. Artwork uses standard sprites and may not always depict Hisuian forms.
